About this prototype

Saved benchmark photos, not a live camera. PASS is provisional: two scratched nuts were missed in the reserved-image test. Defect names are unknown until classification is validated.

Highlighted pixels are model responses, not verified physical damage measurements. There are no calibrated millimetres or certified acceptance limits.

Next actions come from fixed rules. A separate prototype Llama note may appear when a matching local Jetson report is configured. It needs human review and is not a confirmed cause or approved factory procedure. Physical-nut and offline end-to-end validation remain pending.

Profile: EfficientAD-S, FP32; 700 × 700 RGB photos resized to 256 × 256. History counts distinct declared part IDs and survives restarts.
