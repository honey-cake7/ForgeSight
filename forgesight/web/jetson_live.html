About benchmark decisions

Benchmark mode requires a 700 × 700 RGB PNG. It uses the frozen MVTec cutoff and 10% review band. File origin is user-declared, not verified. Other uploads remain REVIEW. Grades describe dataset screening, never physical-part acceptance. In the recorded 111-image holdout, two scratch images fell below the cutoff and two good images exceeded it.
